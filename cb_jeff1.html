Message from Jeff Henney

Football practice was delayed on Wednesday for nearly two hours at the site of the Ermine spring training camp. Chris Cork, star wide receiver, on his way to the locker room, happened to look down and notice a suspicious looking, unknown white substance on the practice field. Patrick Cork immediately suspended practice while the FBI was called in to investigate. After a complete field analysis, the FBI determined that the white substance was actually the goal line. Practice was resumed when the FBI determined that the team would not likely encounter the substance again.
