Kirby Newell broke onto the scene in Cork Bowl 4 - the first true game of the modern era. Over his first 6 seasons, Newell had a league-leading 83 receptions and 27 touchdowns (an average of almost 14 catches and 4 1/2 touchdowns per game) while wearing the famous "Flat Rock 2" jersey, including an MVP performance in Cork Bowl 6 when he finished 15 receptions and 7 touchdowns. His career high 24 receptions in Cork Bowl 8 is still tied for the most catches in a game. He also had a league-high 57 tackles and 8 interceptions in his first six seasons and won an MVP award in Cork Bowl 6. A broken foot prior to Cork Bowl 10 sidelined him for the first time in six years, and then a severe farming accident left him unable to play prior to Cork Bowl 11. After missing three straight years, Newell petitioned the league prior to Cork Bowl 13 to be the league's first offense-only quarterback in history. Newell's petition was granted and in Cork Bowls 13 and 14, Newell has served as his team's quarterback. In Cork Bowl 14, Newell shattered the single-game records for completions and attempts when he completed 62 of 92 passes for 15 touchdowns breaking the previous records of 51 (completions) and 86 (attempts). Despite not playing receiver in 5 years, Newell still ranks 3rd all-time in receptions and 4th in touchdowns.
