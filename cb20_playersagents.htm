Willy Truitt Focusing on Defense in 2010; Takes Shots at Howard

September 28th - Record-holding and sure-fire Hall of Fame Cork Bowl Quarterback Willy Truitt is ready to shine on defense and place offense on the back burner. He'll suit up as a wide-receiver for only the third time in his 14-year career, allowing him to set aside the QB playbook in favor of defensive schemes. Moonshine Gazette reporter B.L. Tomato recently caught up with Truitt at a trading card Expo in Fort Wayne, Indiana.

BLT: How are things going in the Sloth camp?
WT: Very good, I'm very happy to be part of this team. We have a great nucleus, especially with some veterans like myself, Wilson, and Ryan. We'll be able to take some pressure off of Waldrop at the QB position.

BLT: What are your expectations for Waldrop?
WT: I expect him to play like a champion. I think he will settle in nicely once the game gets going, and as long as he doesn't try to do too much, he'll be able to execute for us. I can tell you this, he's been waiting for this day for a long time. He's very hungry for a ring, and it has given us veterans new motivation. I'm glad it'll be him throwing to me, not a scared little boy that can't see over his center.

BLT: Is that a shot at Howard?
WT: You can call it that if you want. I don't expect much from him. He's not cut from the QB cloth, you could say. He's playing that position out of desperation, and I believe his performance will reflect that. The only thing he was ever good at was running, and now that is not a factor. I'm not too worried.

BLT: Wow, very candid response. Obviously no love lost for Howard over the years?
WT: My brother and I spent a lot of time teaching Howard the game of football and made him into the star the Corkbowl franchise could promote. Instead of keeping his head down and staying focused, he went Hollywood. Now he's going to quarterback? Really? I can't wait to see this. It has me focusing on defense more than ever before.

BLT: Really, defense? Offense has always been your strong suit.
WT: Like I implied earlier, there's not much I need to worry about on offense. We have a great core of veterans for DWAL to throw to. He knows the game of football as well as anyone, so he's going to raise some eyebrows. Defense is going to be our opportunity to dominate this game. Greg is going to touch the ball on every play, for those who have watched Greg over the years, know that is going to be a huge weakness for them. It's all about him, all the time. That's dangerous for a QB, he needs to just be quiet and let his ability do the talking, but we all know that won't happen.

BLT: What specific weaknesses will Greg have, that the Sloth will try to exploit?
WT: I won't go into great detail of our game plan, but I'll state the obvious. Greg is not a good decision maker, he chokes in big situations, and he gets frustrated very easily. Not good qualities for a guy that is supposed to be the offensive play caller.

BLT: So you are focusing on defense to capitalize on his weakness?
WT: You could say that. Being a factor on defense is just a new set of challenges for me. I'm not saying I will be knocking guys heads off, but I'll be making some plays and covering some ground you've not seen in recent years. Reggie has been working with me on my timing, angles, instinct, and what not the past few years. I believe it is coming to fruition this year, and there's not a better person to feast off of than Howard. It's time to eat, I can't wait.

BLT: What's for lunch?
WT: No, I was referring to eating Howard's lunch on November 20th.

BLT: Gotcha, good luck in Cork Bowl 20.
WT: Thank you, it will be a great day. Now get out of my face.

Patrick Cork Breaks Down the Matchups

October 11th - Time for some player-by-player matchup breakdowns:

Greg vs. D-Wal - To be honest, I don't have a clue what to expect from these two. The rookie jitters will definitely be a factor. However, Greg knows exactly what to expect in this game - but he's in a completely different element by playing quarterback. I'm not sure that D-Wal doesn't have the advantage coming in being completely new to the whole game. Knowing too much could hurt Greg. But Greg is a winner and a champion - I expect a good game from him as long as he can limit the mistakes and not get too greedy. If our chair baseball careers are any indication, I will OWN D-Wal on November 20th. Although I won't be taking him deep with the bat, I might be taking away his deep throws.

Wilson vs. Trimble - I've been anxiously awaiting this matchup since the teams were announced. Brooksy has been covered by Chris Cork for the majority of the past five years. It's no wonder he's put up huge numbers year in and year out. Not to take away anything from Chris, but defensively, he's not exactly what you would call fleet of foot. In fact, he's had some trouble in training camp keeping up with the tackling dummies in some of our coverage drills, so that's never a good sign. I can't wait to see Trimble draped all over him. To take a phrase out of the Coach Cork book of quotes - he'll be on him like ugly on an ape. And when Trimble gets bored guarding him - I wouldn't mind shutting him down for a while myself. As for Trimble - much like the pictures he has been posting on facebook of his homemade bread, I fully expect him to RISE to the occasion. This guy is primed for an historic game - the single-game receptions record of 24 could be in jeopardy. He is a mismatch nightmare for the Sloth- as long as he and Greg can get the timing down. Anything less than 20 catches would be a shock. I have no idea who they plan to put on him. Is double coverage a possibility? If so, with no safety in play, the Sloth will be opening themselves up to some big plays downfield.

Patrick vs. Willy - I don't know if this matchup will materialize or not, but it's definitely a possibility on both sides of the ball. Willy will definitely have fresh legs having not played receiver in over five years. When he did play, you could put him down for double digits in receptions. I would expect similar numbers again this year, but how will he handle having a rookie quarterback? I can definitely see some animosity building up here with the all-time leading passer taking calls from the newcomer. The lack of physical contact over the past five years could definitely come into play. The last time Willy played receiver, he was still used to taking contact at quarterback from blitzes in prior seasons - it's been a LONG time since this guy has taken a hit. Can he hold up? We'll see. I plan on spending the majority of the game watching Trimble dominate. I'm already adjusted to no longer being the #1 receiver on my team and I'm fine with it. However, I am a little disappointed that I probably won't get to play much safety on defense. I don't remember the last time I had to guard someone and am not looking forward to it at all.

Jake vs. Williamson - Again, I'm not sure how often this matchup will happen, but I'm sure it will on several occasions. Three years ago I would have given the advantage to Williamson, but Jake has become an animal over the past three seasons. Apparently he is still working out, running and lifting weights which as you know is frowned upon by the league office. His 17 catches last year equaled his total from the three previous seasons and he more than doubled his career touchdowns in one game. The Sloth would definitely take another 17 catch/8 touchdown game from him. It wouldn't be surprsing to see Trimble end up on Hoalt at times too. As for Williamson, nobody epitomizes the Cork Bowl spirit like him. However, in a recent practice he was overheard saying, "I've never been fatter." That can't be a good sign unless he's expecting some goal line blocking assignments in our JUMBO package. He missed last year's game for a lady friend (which is not a valid excuse) so he has a ton of pressure on him to perform. You can't call in the night before the game because of a girl and not have pressure the following year. Or, in the case of Kirby Newell, you call in the night before the game to say you can't play and then never show up again. At least Williamson's coming back....supposedly. If nothing else, I expect big things from him in the pregame show once again.

Chris vs. Kevin - I don't know how often these two players will be on the field for defense because they have both been more offensively-minded over the years. I think these two are crucial components of the success of their two teams. I would bet Chris will be on the field almost the entire game on offense - and unless Wilson guards him - he's going to have a size advantage over any of the other Sloth defenders. There were some seasons 5 or 6 years ago when I thought Chris was done - but his last three games (15/7/12 receptions) show that's not the case. He's got to catch at least 10 balls again this year for the Mallards. As for Kevin, he's always an X factor. He can cause a lot of havoc after the catch, but he's got to catch the ball! After catching 15 passes in Cork Bowl 16, he's only caught 17 total since then - the Sloth need him back near double-digits. How long can his body take the punishment? He's had shoulder and hip issues for the majority of his career.

Tomlinson vs. Woodard - this is turning into one of the all-time fan favorite matchups. These two fight like cats and dogs so don't be surprised if there is some pushing and shoving early as they both try to get inside the other's head. If Woodard can get open on offense, he's a load to bring down - but he's got to get some separation from the defense. You know D-Wal is going to love his style of play and I bet they try to get him some early touches. I keep expecting him to breakout and get 8-10 catches in a game but it hasn't happened yet. As for Tomlinson, we all know what this guy brings. He's the heart and soul of every Cork Bowl team he's played on. He's going to make a huge catch at some point in this game that will be a difference maker. And I'm also predicting his third career interception this year.

Zehr vs. Barmes - ah, the two rookies. You never know how a rookie will respond to the bright lights on the big stage. Is one of these guys the next Jeff Henney? Talent-wise, both of these guys belong in the game without a doubt. Will one of them step up and make a huge impact? Will one of them crack under the pressure? Time will tell. If they aren't matched up on each other, they'll likely draw some comination of Wilson, Trimble, the Corks and Hoalt. Their ability to guard multiple players will be a huge asset for both defenses. With the exta sub, fresher legs should lead to better defense late in the game. The ability is there, but the lack of experience could be a huge factor. If one of them steps up more than expected, you have to give the advantage to that team. I expect to see each of them finish with 10+ catches and at least 8 tackles. Both teams need them to be another offensive option and to play well on defense. It will be interesting to see how each team uses these guys - they'll both play most of the game on defense, but the impact on offense is what will be key.

There are way too many unknowns this year to make a score prediction - especially with the two new quarterbacks. If one of them simply doesn't have what it takes to lead a team in Cork Bowl, their team won't have a chance. Three of the last four games haven't been decided until late in the 4th quarter and I would expect this year to be no different. With the extra player per team I bet we see more no-huddle offense and a lot of scoring. I also expect to see more turnovers than recent years with the new QB's.

Willy Truitt Refutes Retirement Talk

November 16th - TRUITT NOT TALKING RETIREMENT, FOCUSED ON WINNING 9TH CHAMPIONSHIP

Through his agent, Cork Bowl Hall-of-Fame star Willy Truitt has officially refuted a pending retirement following reports leaked over the weekend by anonymous league sources. He has released this statement:

"I apologize to my Sloth teammates for this late distraction. We've had a great camp this fall, and I'm excited to see us perform in game speed. There is nothing I am thinking about or that I am focused more on than winning this game. As for the report, I can see how those words could be twisted. I'm not getting any younger, that I will admit. But, the fans should know that the league takes our health and well-being very seriously. They are working very hard behind the scenes to ensure many more years of veteran participation, including myself. I think they are realizing that maybe we can't sell sodas and sneakers anymore, and our demographic is shifting to vitamins and viagra."
