Josh Williamson picked a fine time for his first Cork Bowl when he debuted in the snowstorm known as Cork Bowl 5. Spending half the game at QB, Williamson struggled completing just 8 of 24 passes and moved to receiver full time. After several low reception totals early in his career, Williamson became a very solid option averaging 9.5 receptions a game between Cork Bowls 9 and 13, including a career-high 13 catch game in Cork Bowl 13. Using his low center of gravity, Williamson's biggest asset is running after the catch bouncing around like a bowling ball to get extra yards. Williamson missed Cork Bowl 11 due to health reasons and later missed Cork Bowl 14 after his new job took him to Virginia making him unavailable for the game. Williamson is best known his defensive aggressiveness and is one of the surest tacklers in the game. Williamson tallied a career-high 10 tackles in Cork Bowl 8 including a single-game record 2 sacks. Williamson is the all-time leader in sacks with 4 in his career, and also leads the league in safeties with 2. Unless the league takes away the no-rushing rule, Williamson will forever be the leader in both categories.
