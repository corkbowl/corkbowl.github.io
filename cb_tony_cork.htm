Although it seems like Tony Cork has been involved in Cork Bowl since its inception, he has actually only played 3 full games, and has played in only 6 games total - all at the quarterback position. Cork was a part-time injury replacement at QB in Cork Bowl 4, and filled in at QB for the 4th quarter in the blowout game of Cork Bowl 5. His first full game at quarterback came in Cork Bowl 8 when he was united with both of his sons and led his Lemurs to a win by completing a single-game record 51 passes for 18 touchdowns. Those records would stand for 5 years. After retiring for 3 years, Cork came back for Cork Bowl 12 and was unable to get away from the blitzing defense and completed just 25 of 50 passes and threw 6 interceptions. Cork filled in during Cork Bowl 13 after Reggie Truitt was injured, and with the no-rush rule in effect, Cork sizzled in relief duty completing 35 of 50 passes for 9 touchdowns in just over 2 quarters of play. Cork returned full-time in Cork Bowl 14 and set a single-game record by completing 71% of his passes (51-72) and also broke his own touchdowns mark with 19. Despite only playing 3 full games at QB, Cork ranks 2nd all-time in most passing categories.
