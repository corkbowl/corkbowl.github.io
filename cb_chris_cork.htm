Chris Cork is one of only two players (along with brother Patrick) to participate in every Cork Bowl. Cork spent a little over half of his time at quarterback for his first several seasons before becoming a standout receiver the second half of his career. After a lackluster career throwing the ball, Cork moved to receiver full-time in Cork Bowl 6 where he set a single-game record with 22 receptions. That record stood until Cork Bowl 8. Cork, who has one of the softest pair of hands in the game, ranks second all-time in receptions and touchdowns in his career and is one of only two players to post 100 receptions and 40 touchdowns. Cork was a dominant defender in his early years as he picked off 2 passes in each of his first 4 games. His 12 career interceptions are tied for first all-time. From Cork Bowl 6 through Cork Bowl 14, Cork has averaged over 11 1/2 receptions a year and nearly 5 touchdowns per game - earning him the nickname "King of the Red Zone".
