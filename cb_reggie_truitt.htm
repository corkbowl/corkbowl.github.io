Reggie Truitt debuted in Cork Bowl 2 and also played in Cork Bowl 4. From Cork Bowl 6 on, Truitt has played in every Cork Bowl since. His first several years were spent mostly at the quarterback position including a record-setting day in Cork Bowl 7 when he completed 51 passes on 86 attempts with 18 touchdowns (records which stood until Cork Bowl 14). However, Truitt's record-setting 7 interceptions thrown kept him from winning the MVP award although he led his team to victory. He split most of his time at quarterback and receiver from 1998-2000 before moving to the receiver spot full-time in Cork Bowl 11. Thanks to his time spent at both the quarterback and receiver positions, Truitt ranks 3rd in most passing categories and 5th in receptions. Truitt's finest moment came in Cork Bowl 12 when he picked off a record-setting 5 passes in his team's blowout win. Truitt also had 12 catches and 5 touchdowns on offense to bring home his first ever MVP award. Truitt's 98 tackles and 12 interceptions are #1 all-time, and his confusing blitzing schemes in Cork Bowls 11 and 12 helped lead his team to victory both years before the no-rushing rule was implemented.
