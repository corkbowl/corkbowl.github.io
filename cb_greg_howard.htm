Greg Howard came on the scene in Cork Bowl 6 as a 5'0, 90 pound rookie. Over the years Howard has grown into a force on the field and is now one of the most dominant players in the game. Thanks to his cross country career, Howard has the most stamina of any player in the game and is able to put up good numbers, especially in the second half of games. Howard steadily improved on the offensive side of the ball his first 5 years going from 2 catches in Cork Bowl 6 to 12 catches by Cork Bowl 10 before suffering through a pair of drop-plagued seasons in Cork Bowls 11 and 12 where he combined to catch just 6 passes. However, over the past two seasons Howard has set career highs with 15 catches in Cork Bowl 13 and 18 receptions in Cork Bowl 14. Howard was named MVP of Cork Bowl 13 after his 15 catch, 5 touchdown, 12 tackle game. It was in Cork Bowl 14 where Howard had one of the most amazing individual performances in Cork Bowl history as he tallied a record-shattering 26 tackles (previous record was 16) and also had 18 receptions for 6 touchdowns to share MVP honors - his second honor in as many years. Over the past 4 seasons, Howard has averaged 14.5 tackles a game including 3 games of 12+ tackles. His 7-2 all-time record is the best winning percentage in the history of the game and he was won 7 of his last 8 Cork Bowls.
