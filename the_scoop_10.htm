October 16th- - The "Scoop": The Best in Investigative Sports Reporting

Well, to be honest, I'd forgotten that there still was a Cork Bowl, but I figure I'd better write something. Now that the Chris Cork and Kirby Newell rivalry is gone and both have retired, there isn't much to write about. However, I'll see what I can come up with.

NOTE: (my editor just informed me that Chris Cork hasn't retired, but just stinks now days ala Brett Favre.............What's next, sexual harassment allegations about him??)

I've decided to make a little preview using similes. As a few of my readers might know, a simile is a comparison using "like" or "as". For example, "The Scoop" is as brilliant as Albert Einstein. Or, "The Scoop" writes like a drunk Hemingway. I think that you are beginning to catch on.

Greg Howard: Greg Howard is like an ant. He is small, short, and strong. However, ants are not known to perform well under pressure. For example, ants die under the pressure of my cowboy boot.

Chris Cork: Chris Cork is as slow as a turtle. When I say that, I mean because turtles are slow and Chris is slow.

Robert Trimble: Robert is as shaggy as a yak. I haven't seen Robert in years, but I'm assuming that he hasn't gotten a haircut since then, so therefore he'd be really shaggy nowadays...........like a yak.

Patrick Cork: Patrick is as wylie as a hyena. Many people think that coyotes are wylie, and they are, but they aren't as wylie as hyenas. Why do I say that?? Because hyenas actually steal food from lions and live to tell about it. I don't think a coyote could do that. That being said, I'm no coyote expert and even if I were, there aren't lions here so I wouldn't really know. But, I like to use the word wylie.

Josh Williamson: He is like a wounded bear. However, in this case I'm talking about a Chicago Bear. Specifically a wounded Chicago Bear offensive lineman. We know how bad the Bear's offensive line is and I think I'll just stop there.

Ryan Tomlinson: Ryan is like a skunk. Skunks stink and, quite honestly Ryan does too.

Aaron Zehr: Aaron is like a duckbilled platypus. I've never seen a duckbilled platypus and I've never seen Aaron. I'd like to see a duckbilled platypus in action and I'm sure I'd love to see this guy put on the cleats.

Dustin Waldrop: Dustin is as powerful as an elephant. That is definitely an asset as an offensive/defensive lineman. Or, even as a tight end. But as a QB, although does it cause you to overthrow your receivers. Granted, elephants are know to have great memories. So, perhaps Dustin will know the play book forwards and backwards. Then again, perhaps a mouse will run out on the field and Dustin will cry like a baby. Dustin does get extra credit for wearing a cowboy hat in his picture and I think that is cool. Some say that I am the epitome of cool, so take my opinion for what it is worth.

Brooks Wilson: Brooks is as sneaky as a snake. I'm not sure if snakes are really sneaky. But, I do know that the great Tom T. Hall wrote a song called "Sneaky Snake". In the song, Sneaky Snake goes dancin' and drinks all of my root beer. I've seen Brooks dance, but only after he has had a few root beers. Anywho, if Tom T. Hall says that snakes are sneaky, who am I to argue. I do know that Brooks is sneaky..................like a snake. Did you notice that I used both a simile and a metaphor in that paragraph?

Willy Truitt: Willy is as fierce as a mole. Most people don't know that moles are fierce, but they are. If you don't believe me, you can ask my brother. When I was young, there was a mole out in our yard. I was sick and I asked my brother to go get it for me. Being the nice brother that he is, he went and got it for me. As he picked it up, the blind son of a gun bit him on the finger. He had to go to the doctor. That is fierceness if I've ever seen it. Besides, you have to be fierce to dig tunnels the way that moles do.

Kevin Ryan: Kevin is like a skunk ape. It is a little known fact that skunk apes are of the sasquatch family. The skunk ape is specific to to the Southern United States. Skunk Apes often appear and then disappear for long periods of time. So does Kevin on the football field.

Jake Hoalt: Jake is as dangerous as a Great White Shark. We all know that Jake is a future MMA star, a natural born killer. So are Great White Sharks. But, have you ever seen a shark catch a football. NO!!!! They have no hands................and neither does Jake!

Evan Woodard: Evan is as confused as a concussed catfish. Let me confess, I don't know as though I know Evan or have ever seen him play. But, I do know catfish and based on Evan's picture, he is as confused as a concussed one. Catfish eat stinkbait, chicken livers, and other stinky things. In order to eat those things, you have to be confused. Imagine how confused a concussed catfish is!! Seriously, imagine that. Then you will understand Evan, or so I think.

Evan Barmes: Barmes is like a praying mantis. He is praying that he doesn't get dominated in his rookie debut. He wouldn't be the first rookie to be dominated, but he might be the last. Why, because the Cork Brothers are old as dirt and this game can't keep going on forever. Getting back to Barmes, the praying mantis is an awesome insect. He eats annoying pests. I wouldn't be surprised if he eats some of the annoying pests on the opposing team.

Now for the prediction!!!!!!!

I pick the Sloths, no the Sloth, no the Slothi, I mean Dustin's team to win. Why??????

Because the Sloth defense will handcuff Greg. Get it, they will handcuff the policeman!!!!!!!!!!!

Signing off,

"Scoop"
