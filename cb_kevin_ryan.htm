Kevin Ryan is one of Cork Bowl's true warriors - always wearing his emotions on his sleeve. Ryan picked up a win in his first Cork Bowl in Cork Bowl 4, and then proceeded to lose 4 consecutive times. After playing in Cork Bowls 4 and 5, Ryan missed the next two years after a freak injury, but has been on the field each season since Cork Bowl 8. In Cork Bowls 9 and 10, Ryan split time at quarterback completing 38 of 69 passes for 15 touchdowns. He moved back to receiver for Cork Bowl 11 and averaged nearly 8 catches and 2.5 touchdowns per game. Ryan nearly won the MVP award in the muddy Cork Bowl 11 leading his team with a career-high 13 catches. Ryan also scored 4 TD's, had an interception, 1 1/2 sacks and 1/2 safety in his team's win, but was edged out in the voting by Jeff Henney. One of the games only true tight ends, Ryan loves contact and uses his bruising style to rumble for extra yards. After struggling early in his career to get a win, Ryan has won 3 of the last 4 years to move his all-time record to 4-5.
