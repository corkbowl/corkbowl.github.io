Rookie Henney Responds to Recent Remarks

Willy,

I am glad to see that you have bought into my game plan, "Low expectations = High satisfaction." However, if you will be satisfied with only 4 receptions, 1 TD, and 0 fumbles from me, prepare yourself to be blown away. I do not say this to change our game plan; rather, I think it is important to adjust our expectations, while keeping them low, at least into the realm of reality.

Also, in preparation for the Cork Bowl, I feel quite fortunate to be working every day at the hospital. Just last week, I sneaked into the pharmacy and started myself on an anti-fear concoction/tonic. I can feel the effects already. You can upgrade me to probable, I think. Fortunately, one of the side effects is "sticky hands." Further, I am glad the time for drug testing has passed - I don't think the Bandicoots want to play this one 4 on 5 (though that might make the game a bit more competitive).

As far as death threats go, I apologize for that comment. I could have sworn the "Scoop" said that would be off the record; I guess I need to learn to be more savvy with the press. As a rookie, I just feel fortunate to have the chance to play with such an elite quarterback; I'll try to keep my mouth shut. I do not plan to disappoint, nor do I plan to kill - only malign.

--Jeff
