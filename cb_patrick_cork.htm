Patrick Cork is one of just two players to participate in every Cork Bowl (along with brother Chris). Patrick's career began when he was 12 years old, and after a few decent outings, he broke out in Cork Bowl 6 with 17 catches and had a run until Cork Bowl 12 that has never been approached by any other player. Over that 6 year period, Patrick caught 112 passes (an average of 18.7 each year) and scored 36 touchdowns (6 per year). He broke the single-game receiving record in Cork Bowl 8 with 24 receptions and later tied the mark in Cork Bowl 11, when he also set the single-game touchdown mark with 8 scores. A broken collarbone in Cork Bowl 12 in the first quarter forced him from the game with just 3 receptions, but he bounced back with 10 catches in Cork Bowl 13 and 21 last year. He is the only player to have over 150 catches and 50 touchdowns. His 164 career catches are almost 50 more than the next player. He is a 4-time MVP winner, taking home the honors in Cork Bowls 8 and 9 and sharing honors in Cork Bowls 10 (Willy Truitt) and 14 (Greg Howard). His 7-4 career record is second best all-time.
