Greg Howard Sounds Off Again

In the recent days there have been numerous remarks made about Bandicoot players. Some involve that of legendary QB Willy Truitt's injury, which I would like to go on record by saying that sounds like it really hurts, but it's good to see Willy is going to be a real man and play through the pain. Others involve the rookie Jeff Henney, who I have no doubt will be a major force in the game (just as long as he doesn't drop 15 passes like Sanders did last year). Oh, and one of the most important ones is that of the name of our team. Crash Bandicoot was an awesome game, it might not rank with that of Donkey Kong or Mortal Kombat but it's definitely a new classic. I would also like to take this time to point out that Ryan Tomlinson aka "powerhouse killer" is a wimp. The only thing he kills is his team's chance of winning. I'm making a personal committment to destroying him this year. Also, I recently visited Josh Williamson and he has bulked up to a huge 195, his chest is massive, so watch out for his raw power on the field.

P.S. I haven't commented on the retirement of Kirby Newell and may I say that I'm deeply saddened by it. He was a player everyone looked up to, some of us more than others. He was a man's man. To show my appreciation to Kirby I will be dedicating my performance to his memory. I just hope it's a good one.

Greg (Tomilinson Killer) Howard
