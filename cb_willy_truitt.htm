Willy Truitt's first game played was in Cork Bowl 2. At the ripe age of 10, and measuring in at about 4'8, 70 pounds, Truitt was a late fill-in on brother Reggie's team. Truitt returned to the field 4 years later for Cork Bowl 6, where he began what would be an historic run as the league's preeminent quarterback. Truitt started at quarterback for 8 consecutive years (CB 6 thru 13), winning 5 times. Over that time, he completed 271 of 472 passes (57.4%) for 105 touchdowns and had just 15 interceptions. In his 6 full games at QB, from Cork Bowl 7 through Cork Bowl 12, Truitt averaged 38.8 completions and 15 touchdowns a game. Truitt shared MVP honors with Patrick Cork in Cork Bowl 10 when he completed 44 of 71 passes for 16 touchdowns while also intercepting 2 passes on defense. In the middle of Cork Bowl 13, Truitt was moved to receiver after Reggie was injured and caught 12 passes in just 2 1/2 quarters. Kirby Newell and Tony Cork were named as quarterbacks for Cork Bowl 14 so Truitt played his first full game at receiver since his debut 12 years earlier, and caught a career-high 14 passes for 3 touchdowns. Truitt is the all-time leader in completions, attempts, touchdowns and rushing touchdowns.
