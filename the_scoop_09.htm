November 15th- - The "Scoop": The Best in Investigative Sports Reporting

Well, after all these years of Cork Bowl, it is time that somebody came out and said it!!! Cork Bowl isn't what it used to be. 10 years ago, Cork Bowl was the equivalent of a match up between the '85 Bears and the 1999 Rams. Those are 2 of the best teams of all time and would have been an amazing game. Cork Bowl used to have fizzle. It used to have stars (aka Kirby Newell and Reggie Truitt). Now, we have the modern day Cleveland Browns vs. Detroit Lions. Sure, there are people who will watch, but most of those people will fall asleep.

Honestly, who wants to watch Chris Cork (who has been in decline since 1989) against Evan Woodard. Seriously, I don't even know who that kid is. And, what's up with that hair. It looks like a poodle dyed its hair and then took a nap on the kid's head. Kevin Ryan's been around so long that when he first started out there was hair on his head. Patrick Cork is getting slower than Kirby Newell was in his first cross country meet (and that was slow). I just don't get why people keep putting themselves through the misery of playing in a Cork Bowl.

On the other hand, lets talk about these pictures of Greg Howard (aka Hefty Smurf). Seriously, did you see his legs!!!............I mean, what is up with that??? He says that those are Halloween pictures, but we all know that you don't wear purple and black after Labor Day!!! Plus, if those are Halloween photos, why wouldn't he have a plastic pumpkin to carry his candy? An even better question is this, why is he, at 28ish, still trick or treating? Sure, he could pass as a 7th grader, but that is just ridiculous. Nobody his age should still be dressing up for Halloween unless they are running the chainsaw at the Jaycees Haunted House!! The guy just needs to stop. I'm really thinking he needs to move to a new country where he'd fit in better. Let's see here, where is a place where Hefty would fit in...............hmmmm.........where are Shetland ponies from....................anybody.........anybody........... Well, maybe Chris Cork can get back to us on that one.

By the way Hefty, watch out for Gargamell!

Ryan Tomlinson???? I thought that kid was dead or in jail or perhaps in hibernation for the last 10 years. I just don't get that guy. Does he have a job? Maybe a sugar momma? Who knows, but we really don't need anymore of that guy do we? Also, it is just the picture or does his head look huge in the game preview picture?

Well, I've gotta a Stomper pulling contest to get to, so you guys take care.

Signing off,

"Scoop"
